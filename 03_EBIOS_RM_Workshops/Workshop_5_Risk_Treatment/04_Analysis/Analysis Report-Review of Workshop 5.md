# Workshop 5 – Risk Treatment
## Consistency Review Report

**Project:** AI-Enabled Supply Chain Management Information System (AI-SCMIS)  
**Framework:** EBIOS Risk Manager  
**Review scope:** Consistency between Workshop 4 – Operational Scenarios and Workshop 5 – Risk Treatment

### 1. Objective of the Review

The objective of this review is to verify the coherence and consistency between the results of Workshop 4 (Operational Scenarios) and Workshop 5 (Risk Treatment).

The review focuses on ensuring that the risks identified and assessed in Workshop 4 are correctly carried forward into Workshop 5, and that the proposed treatment strategies and security measures remain consistent with the initial risk assessment.

### 2. Review Results

#### 2.1 Scenario Identification

**Status: Consistent**

The review confirmed that the operational scenarios identified in Workshop 4 are correctly represented in Workshop 5.

- All 28 operational scenarios have corresponding risk entries.
- Risk identification and numbering maintain consistency between the two workshops.
- No missing or additional risks were identified during the review.

The traceability between operational scenarios and their corresponding risks is therefore maintained.

#### 2.2 Risk Scoring and Classification

**Status: Consistent**

The review confirmed that the initial risk scores and treatment classifications established in Workshop 4 have been correctly transferred into Workshop 5.

The distribution remains as follows:

| Treatment classification | Number of risks |
|---|---:|
| Avoid | 14 |
| Reduce | 14 |
| Accept | 0 |
| Neglect | 0 |
| **Total** | **28** |

No discrepancies were identified in the transfer of risk scores or their corresponding classifications.

#### 2.3 Treatment Strategy (Avoid / Reduce)

**Status: Pending team discussion**

During the review, a potential inconsistency was identified between the treatment classifications established in Workshop 4 and some of the security measures proposed in Workshop 5.

In particular, certain risks classified as **Avoid** have treatment measures that primarily aim to reduce the likelihood or impact of the corresponding scenarios, rather than eliminate the underlying activity or exposure.

This observation raises a methodological question regarding the distinction between risk avoidance and risk reduction.

The matter will be discussed during a team meeting to determine whether adjustments to the treatment strategies or their associated measures are necessary.

No changes to the existing risk classifications or treatment decisions are proposed at this stage. They remain subject to the team's discussion and validation.

### 3. Overall Conclusion

The consistency review confirmed that Workshop 5 maintains coherence with Workshop 4 regarding risk identification, traceability, scoring and classification.

The main point requiring further consideration concerns the appropriateness of the proposed treatment strategies, particularly the distinction between Avoid and Reduce.

This issue will be submitted to the team for discussion and collective decision-making before the final consolidation of Workshop 5.
