## Responsibility pattern

- **Coordinator:** owns the integrated plan, meeting cadence, action follow-up, GitHub access, and escalation of schedule or scope issues.
- **Workshop lead(s):** coordinate the preparation and completion of each workshop’s tasks and submit a concise output pack for review.
- **Workshop contributors:** gather evidence, complete assigned analysis, document decisions and assumptions, and review peers’ work.
- **Editors/reductors:** maintain the common report structure, consolidate approved outputs, check traceability and consistency, and draft the ISSP/BCP updates after workshop results are available.
- **All members:** review key outputs and keep changes in the shared version-controlled repository.
