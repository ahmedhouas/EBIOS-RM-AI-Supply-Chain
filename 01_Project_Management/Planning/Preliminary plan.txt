## Purpose and outcomes

Run an EBIOS-RM analysis of the supply-chain case, including relevant AI use and workflows. Produce outputs from the five workshops, consolidate them into a risk-analysis report, then update/reduce the Information System Security Policy (ISSP) and Business Continuity Plan (BCP). The team agreed to focus on the most important parts of the case rather than attempt to cover everything.## Overall planning basis

- **Indicative duration:** approximately four months, as discussed in the meeting. This is a planning estimate, not a confirmed baseline.
- **Workflow:** preparation → Workshops 1–5 → consolidated report and validation → ISSP/BCP updates → final review and submission.
- **Workshop order:**
    1. Framing and security base
    2. Sources of risk
    3. Strategic scenarios
    4. Operational scenarios
    5. Risk treatment
- Exact dates for Workshops 2–5 and later deliverables were not set. Confirm them after Workshop 1 and record them in the project schedule.
